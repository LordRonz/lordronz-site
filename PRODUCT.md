# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors evaluating Aaron Christopher's work, writing, experience, and
interests. The primary visitor segment is intentionally undecided.

## Product Purpose

Aaron Christopher's personal website and portfolio. It should quickly introduce
him, make his work and background easy to explore, and provide direct paths to
his About page, CV, projects, writing, and public profiles.

## Positioning

The site represents a programmer who works across backend technologies, system
administration, automation with Python, React, and Swift, and who publishes the
website itself as open source.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing factual homepage content and routes should remain intact.
- The homepage hero composition and decorative graphic may be redesigned.
- The landing page must remain responsive, accessible, theme-aware, and
  materially lighter than the previous React Three Fiber/WebGL graphic.
- The existing Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind stack remains in place.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Aaron Christopher.
- The current direct, personal voice and pink-red primary accent are
  recognizable existing traits.
- Existing factual claims must not be expanded or embellished without evidence.

## Evidence on Hand

- Homepage introduction and calls to action in
  `src/components/pages/HomePage.tsx`.
- About, CV, projects, blog, and components routes.
- Public source repository linked from the homepage.
- Existing site assets under `public/` and `src/assets/`.

## Product Principles

- Let real work and factual content lead.
- Make the first viewport memorable without making it slow.
- Prefer direct navigation and legible hierarchy over ornamental complexity.
- Treat accessibility and performance as part of the portfolio's credibility.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic navigation, keyboard access, visible focus states, sufficient
target sizes, reduced-motion support, and WCAG-compatible names and contrast.
